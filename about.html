About this site

Why we built it

Every year, millions of people plug an iPhone into a Windows PC and discover their photos won't open. HEIC is a great format for saving space — and a frustrating one when a website, a print shop or an old program demands a JPG. We wanted a converter that just works: no sign-up walls, no watermarks, no "you've reached your free limit", and no uploading personal photos to a stranger's server.

How it works

The entire converter runs in your browser via WebAssembly. Your images are decoded and re-encoded locally, on your own device. You can verify this yourself: open the tool, disconnect from the internet, and it will keep converting.

The site is free — how?

The site costs almost nothing to run (static hosting, no servers processing your images), so unobtrusive banner advertising covers the costs. That's the whole business model: no paid tiers, no feature locking, no data selling.

What MyHeicGo offers

MyHeicGo covers the four conversions people actually need after taking photos on an iPhone:

  • HEIC to JPG — the everyday choice: small files that open anywhere.
  • HEIC to PNG — lossless quality for screenshots, design work and transparency.
  • HEIC to WebP — a modern format that balances quality and size for the web.
  • HEIC to PDF — bundle several photos into one tidy, printable document.

Beyond the converter itself, we publish plain-English guides that answer the questions we kept seeing: HEIC vs JPG explained, how to open HEIC files on Windows, how to make your iPhone take JPG photos instead, batch-converting a whole album, and more.

Our principles

Privacy is the default, not a premium feature. Many online converters work by uploading your photos to their servers — sometimes keeping them for days. We think photos of your family, your home and your receipts should never have to leave your hands, so the conversion engine was built to run entirely on your device.

Free means free. No accounts to create, no watermarks stamped on your images, no "3 conversions per day" countdown, no email address required. Advertising keeps the lights on so the tool never has to nag you.

Fast and honest. No fake progress bars, no redirects to unrelated sites, no pop-ups over the download button. The page loads once and does exactly one job.

Who is behind MyHeicGo?

MyHeicGo is built and maintained by a small independent team of developers who kept running into the same wall: every "free" HEIC converter we tried either wanted an account, queued our photos on a slow server, or buried the download button under ads. We figured that a converter with four real features and zero dark patterns would be genuinely useful to a lot of people — so we built one.

The project is actively maintained: the conversion engine is updated as Apple evolves the HEIC format (newer iPhone models introduced variants that older converters can't read), and user feedback decides what gets built next.
