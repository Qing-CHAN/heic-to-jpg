Frequently asked questions

How is the PDF laid out?

Each photo becomes one A4 page, scaled to fit. Files are added in the order you dropped them.

Are my documents uploaded?

No. Conversion and PDF assembly happen entirely in your browser. Disconnect from the internet after the page loads — it still works.

Can I reorder pages?

Remove a page with its ✕ button and add files back in the order you want. Keep it simple: drop files in the sequence you need.
