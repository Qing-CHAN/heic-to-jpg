Why convert HEIC to PDF?

PDF is the standard format for sending documents that must look the same on every device. Turning your HEIC photos into a PDF is perfect for homework submissions, expense reports, scanned-receipt replacements, portfolios and anything you need to print or email as a single file.

Instead of emailing five separate pictures, you attach one PDF with every photo in order. If you just need the pictures themselves, converting HEIC to JPG or HEIC to PNG gives you individual image files instead.

What makes a good photo PDF?

Correct order: pages appear in the order you added the photos, so add them in the sequence you want them read. No surprise compression: photos are embedded at high quality (92%), not squashed to thumbnails. Consistent pages: every page uses the size you picked — A4, Letter, or the photo's exact original dimensions — so nothing shifts or stretches. And because the PDF is created on your device, its contents are known only to you.

A typical use: photograph five pages of a paper form, drop them in, choose A4, download one PDF — it looks like a real scan and is accepted almost everywhere a scan is.

Frequently asked questions

Are my photos uploaded to a server?

No. Everything — decoding and PDF creation — runs entirely inside your browser using WebAssembly. Your files never leave your device.

Can I put several photos in one PDF?

Yes — drop as many as you like. Each photo becomes one page, in the order you added them, and you download the whole document with one button.

Which page size should I choose?

Pick A4 or US Letter for printing and sharing. Pick "Original size" when you want each page to exactly match the photo's pixels with no borders.
