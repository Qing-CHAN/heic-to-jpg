Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Your files never leave your device

This website converts HEIC images to JPG entirely inside your browser using WebAssembly. The files you select are never uploaded to any server, never transmitted over the network during conversion, and never stored by us. When you close the page, nothing remains.

Information we do not collect

We do not require accounts, do not ask for personal information, and do not see, store or process the images you convert.

Server logs

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any website, GitHub's servers may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages visited) for security and operational purposes. See GitHub Pages documentation and the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

Cookies and third-party advertising

This site is supported by advertising. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info.

Information we do not collect

To be as concrete as possible: we do not ask for your name or email to use the converter, we do not create user accounts, we do not receive copies of the images you convert, and we do not sell or share any information with data brokers. The converter has no database of user files because, architecturally, there is nowhere for one to exist.

Local storage on your device

The converter may store a small amount of technical data in your browser (such as a cached copy of the conversion engine) so that repeat visits load faster. This cache stays on your device and never contains your images or personal data. Clearing your browser's site data removes it completely.

Your rights (GDPR and similar laws)

If you are in the European Union, the United Kingdom, or a region with comparable privacy laws, you have the right to access, correct or delete any personal data held about you, and to object to or restrict processing. Because MyHeicGo does not maintain user accounts or databases, the only data that could relate to you is standard web-server log data held by our hosting provider — see their privacy statement linked above for how to exercise your rights with them. For anything else, contact us via the About page.

Children's privacy

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? See the About page for how to reach us.
