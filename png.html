Why convert HEIC to PNG?

PNG is the universal format for images that need to look exactly the same everywhere: it uses lossless compression, supports transparent backgrounds, and opens in every browser, design tool and office program. If you are uploading artwork, screenshots or documents where crispness matters more than file size, PNG is the safer choice than JPG.

The trade-off: PNG files are noticeably larger. If you just want photos that open anywhere, converting HEIC to JPG will keep them much smaller.

Frequently asked questions

Are my photos uploaded to a server?

No. Conversion runs entirely inside your browser using WebAssembly. Your files never leave your device.

PNG or JPG — which should I pick?

Pick PNG for graphics, screenshots, transparency and maximum quality. Pick JPG for regular photos where smaller file size matters.

Can I convert multiple files at once?

Yes — drop as many as you like, then download them one by one or all together in a ZIP.
