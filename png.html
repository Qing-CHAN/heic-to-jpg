Why convert HEIC to PNG?

PNG is the universal format for images that need to look exactly the same everywhere: it uses lossless compression, supports transparent backgrounds, and opens in every browser, design tool and office program. If you are uploading artwork, screenshots or documents where crispness matters more than file size, PNG is the safer choice than JPG.

The trade-off: PNG files are noticeably larger. If you just want photos that open anywhere, converting HEIC to JPG will keep them much smaller.

Real-world uses for HEIC to PNG

Design and editing: design tools treat PNG as the safe import format — colors stay exact and there is no second-generation compression when you re-save your work. Screenshots with text: JPG's compression blurs thin text slightly; PNG keeps small labels razor-sharp. Logos and stickers: PNG supports transparency, so your image blends onto any background. And archiving anything important: a lossless file you store today will look identical in ten years.

Note that transparency only survives if the original HEIC had an alpha channel (some app-generated HEICs do). Regular camera photos become a normal PNG with a white or solid background, exactly matching the photo.

PNG or JPG — which should I pick?

Pick PNG for graphics, screenshots, transparency and maximum quality. Pick JPG for regular photos where smaller file size matters.
